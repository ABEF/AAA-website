A FEW IMPORTANT DETAILS

Understand
what you own.

Can I withdraw the locked liquidity?

No. The core concept makes principal non-redeemable. Authorized rebalancing or migration can move attributed capital, but ownership of an NFT does not create a principal-withdrawal right.

Can I add more liquidity to my NFT?

Yes. After activation, the holder can add more LP to the same NFT. Every top-up increases that NFT's permanent position and the collection total. All added capital remains locked forever.

What happens when I sell my NFT?

The buyer inherits its economic rights, including unclaimed rewards. The underlying liquidity remains in permanent custody. Rewards are associated with the NFT, not a previous owner's wallet.

Are trading fees or returns guaranteed?

No. Fees depend on trading activity and productive liquidity. Token prices, impermanent loss, execution costs, governance, oracles, bridges, and contract failures can affect or destroy value.

Is the protocol live?

No. This website is a development preview using illustrative values. The v0.2 source candidate still requires Solidity verification, real Uniswap integration tests, a production bridge transport, and an independent security audit.

What do the NFT levels represent?

The illustrations propose Dormant ($0), Online ($500), Enhanced ($1,000), Advanced ($5,000), Elite ($25,000), and Singularity ($100,000). This demo illustrates that concept; levels and standalone dormant minting are not implemented in the current contract candidate.
