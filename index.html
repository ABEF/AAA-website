BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few essentials.

Read the protocol source ↗
Does rarity increase my LP rewards?

No. Rarity affects NFT-sale royalty shares only. Commun ×1, Rare ×2 and Legendary ×3 are weights in a separate pool of NFT-sale royalties actually received. Only activated NFTs participate; dormant NFTs have zero royalty weight. Rarity does not change your LP fee share, and these weights do not multiply the marketplace royalty rate.

Can I add more liquidity to the same NFT?

Yes. After activation, you can contribute more ZZZ or USDC to increase the permanent LP allocation attached to that same NFT and the collection total. Its rarity and color stay the same. Added principal remains nonredeemable; new contributions do not earn fees from before they were added.

What happens when I sell or transfer my NFT?

Its attached LP allocation, unclaimed funded rewards and future fee rights follow the NFT to its new owner. The liquidity stays in the protocol. The holder can claim funded rewards, but cannot redeem the LP principal.

What do the NFT evolutions mean?

Dormant starts with $0 LP and no reward eligibility. In this preview, activation begins at $100 in value, Enhanced at $1,000 of attached LP and Singularity at $10,000. The artwork evolves with the attached LP; its rarity and color remain fixed. Minting alone does not add liquidity.

Can I withdraw the locked capital?

No. NFT ownership does not create a principal-withdrawal right. Authorized maintenance or migration may move protocol liquidity, while the capital remains committed.

Are rewards or returns guaranteed?

No. Claims require funded revenue. Trading activity, prices, impermanent loss, execution costs and contract risks affect outcomes. Neither LP rewards nor NFT-sale royalty payments are guaranteed.

Is this live?

This is a simulation with example balances. No contracts are deployed, and the website never signs or submits transactions. Production LP integration, revenue funding, bridge transport, operating tests and independent audit remain outstanding. Dynamic metadata and NFT-sale royalty distribution are proposed features.
